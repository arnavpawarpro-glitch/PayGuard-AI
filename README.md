\# 🛡️ PayGuard AI



\## AI-Powered Payment Risk Manager



PayGuard AI is a prototype payment risk assessment system designed to identify potentially suspicious transactions and provide an understandable risk assessment.



\## 🎯 Problem



Digital payment systems process large numbers of transactions every day. Detecting suspicious behavior quickly while minimizing unnecessary friction for legitimate users is a major challenge.



PayGuard AI addresses this by evaluating multiple transaction signals and converting them into an interpretable risk score.



\## 💡 Solution



PayGuard AI analyzes:



\- Transaction amount

\- Device behavior

\- Transaction frequency

\- International transaction status

\- Failed payment attempts

\- Account age



The system generates:



\- Risk score from 0–100

\- Low / Medium / High risk classification

\- Risk factors

\- AI-assisted explanation

\- Recommended action

\- Recent transaction history
